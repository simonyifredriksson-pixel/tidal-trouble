# Hooked

A chaotic low-poly fishing adventure for 1-4 players, in the browser.

Go fishing. Catch weird things. Upgrade your boat. Explore dangerous waters. Try not to get eaten.

- First-person fishing with a physical rod: cast, wait, strike, fight, reel in.
- Around 50 species across five regions - Driftwood Bay, Frostbite Lake, the Sunken Coast, the Open Sea and the Blackwater - including Bombfish, Thieffish, Mimicfish, Electric Eels, Sharkfish and Ghostfish.
- Giant world-event fish and twelve leviathans, each hidden behind a chain of clues.
- Four boats and seven upgrade tracks, paint and decorations. Everything you catch is a physical object on your deck.
- A cabin that fills up with your trophies, photos and the skulls of the things you caught.
- Storms, migrations, whirlpools, meteors and a boat thief called Pete.
- Co-op for up to four players over WebRTC (PeerJS).

Everything - terrain, trees, buildings, characters, fish, boats, icons and sound - is generated in code. There are no asset files.

Controls: WASD move, mouse look, hold left mouse to cast / reel, click to strike, A/D to pull against a fish, E interact, F pick up, 1-9 tools, B bait, J journal, M map.
